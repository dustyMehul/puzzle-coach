# Puzzles

Interview-puzzle practice with adaptive difficulty. Run it with `/puzzle`. Settings: `puzzle-config.yml` (in `.claude/` or the project root), or ask with `/puzzle config`.

**Current level: 6**

## Difficulty
1–3 easy · 4–5 medium · 6–7 medium-hard · 8–9 hard · 10 very hard

| Result | Meaning | Level |
|---|---|---|
| Solved | right answer, sound reasoning, no hints | +1 |
| Partial | right with a small hint, a missing argument, or an arithmetic slip | 0 |
| Not solved | big hint, solution, or wrong answer | −1 |
